Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 August 2026

Metalift Solutions is committed to handling your information transparently and responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our website, applications, and services.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details such as your name, email address, telephone number, and company when you contact us or submit a form.
  • Information you choose to provide while using our applications or discussing a project with us.
  • Technical and usage information such as device type, browser, IP address, diagnostics, and interactions with our services.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, maintain, secure, and improve our applications, website, and services.
  • To respond to enquiries, provide support, schedule consultations, and deliver proposals.
  • To communicate important service updates and, where you have consented, relevant company news.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and protect our users and rights.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share only the information necessary with trusted providers that help us operate our services, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and safety.

4. Data security and retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards to protect personal information. We retain information only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to provide our services, and to meet legal requirements.

5. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of your personal information. You may also object to certain processing or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and limited analytics to understand aggregate usage and improve our services. You can control cookies using your browser settings.

7. Children's privacy

Our general services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

8. International transfers

Where information is processed outside your country, we use appropriate safeguards in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will publish changes on this page and revise the effective date above.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email info@metaliftsolutions.co.ke or contact Metalift Solutions, Nairobi, Kenya.
